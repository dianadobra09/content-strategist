How does a post get made?

Before the first post

You give it your old posts and a few voice memos, so it learns how you write and talk.

Then, for each post

  1. 01You send your notesA voice memo or a link is fine too.
  2. 02It asks for what's missingIt also checks each fact against its source.
  3. 03It drafts the LinkedIn postIn your words, with a visual if the post needs one.
  4. 04It runs a humanizer and a voice checkThey catch the phrases that sound like AI.
  5. 05It writes the X, Threads and Bluesky versionsEach one is shorter and makes one point.
  6. 06You approve it and Zernio schedules itThe LinkedIn post always goes out first.

When you edit a draft, it learns from your changes.
