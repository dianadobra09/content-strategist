EU AI Act, Article 50

Marking AI-generated content

AI that generates text, images, audio or video has to mark its output as AI-generated, in a machine-readable way.

  1. From 2 Aug 2026AI systems put on the market from that day
  2. From 2 Dec 2026AI systems already on the market before 2 August
  3. ExemptAI that only does standard editinglike fixing grammar and spelling

Source: EU AI Act, Article 50(2), and the European Commission's FAQ on Article 50.
