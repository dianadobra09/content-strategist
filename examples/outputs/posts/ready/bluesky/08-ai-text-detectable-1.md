from today, the EU's rule on detectable AI text also covers products that were already on the market.

i've been dealing with LLMs quite a bit lately, and I have to say, making generated text detectable as AI is no walk in the park. anyone else building with LLMs dealing with this?

Alt text 2: EU AI Act, Article 50: AI that generates text, images, audio or video has to mark its output as AI-generated, in a machine-readable way. From 2 August 2026 for AI systems put on the market from that day; from 2 December 2026 for systems already on the market before then. Exempt: AI that only does standard editing, like fixing grammar and spelling.
