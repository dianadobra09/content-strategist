your AI summary tends to miss important customer feedback.

think about the interviews or support tickets that you paste into ChatGPT to decide what to build next.

a paper from last year had 18 models summarize what 3,000 people said about policy questions.

every single one represented the majority views better than the minority ones.

and that's how valuable customer feedback is lost.
