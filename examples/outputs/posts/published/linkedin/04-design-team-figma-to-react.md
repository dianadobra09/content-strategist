we’re trying to get the design team to replace 80% of their Figma with a React repo.

right now the designers work in Figma, and engineers rebuild their designs in code.

the plan:
→ Figma stays for ideation and prototyping
→ each Figma component becomes a React component, styled with Tailwind
→ designers update those components with Claude or Codex
→ they design screens and flows by telling the AI what to build, using the core components and a well set up design system

to be clear: this is not AI creating visuals, it's designers driving the AI to implement exactly their vision.

it would be amazing for engineers too. the designs would already be in code, so they’d be much easier to implement.

it could also save the company money on subscriptions and help the designers move faster.

the idea sounds good, but I am not sure how it would scale.

does anyone have first-hand experience with this?
