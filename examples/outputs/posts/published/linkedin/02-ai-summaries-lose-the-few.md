your AI summary tends to miss important customer feedback.

think about the interviews or support tickets that you paste into ChatGPT to decide what to build next. Even if the answer is in there, it might be something only one or two people said.

a paper from last year (Stanford, MIT and others) had 18 models summarize what 3,000 people said about policy questions. Every single one represented the majority views better than the minority ones.

so instead of a summary, I’d ask the AI to:
→ go through the feedback one interview or ticket at a time
→ pull out every point, in the customer’s own words
→ group them and count how many people said each one

that way the things only one or two customers mentioned are right there, and I can read those myself.

what’s the most useful thing only one customer told you?
