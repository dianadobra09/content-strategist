# Examples

Real results from Diana Dobra's LinkedIn, X, Threads and Bluesky, made with this workspace. They show the format and the process. The skills use them as references only, never as your posts, results or voice. Paths inside these files are relative to this folder.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `voice/voice-guide.md` | Diana's voice as the workspace learned it: rules from her feedback, observed patterns, asides, tone. |
| `voice/kill-list.md` | The words, openers, constructions and endings Diana rejected, many with the date and her own words. |
| `voice/feedback-log.md` | A selection of the log: each correction, what changed and the rule it added. |
| `outputs/posts/published/linkedin/01-ai-making-us-basic.md` | An explainer built on four studies. Its idea is in `ideas/used/2026-10-12-ai-making-us-basic.md`, its X, Threads and Bluesky versions in the other channel folders. |
| `outputs/posts/published/linkedin/02-ai-summaries-lose-the-few.md` | A short post built on one paper, with its X, Threads and Bluesky versions. |
| `outputs/posts/published/linkedin/03-character-ai-bankruptcy.md` | A research post. `ideas/used/2026-10-21-character-ai-bankruptcy.md` keeps the whole trail: the raw notes, the brief, every fact with its source (and the notes' mistakes corrected), the hooks with their mobile cut, and the edit log. |
| `outputs/posts/published/linkedin/04-design-team-figma-to-react.md` | A post from Diana's own work, with an animated visual in BlinkWrite's brand style. |
| `outputs/visuals/design-team-figma-to-react/` | The animation for post 04: `build-animation.py` writes `index.html`, `render-animation.cjs` renders the MP4 and the still, and `asset-sources.json` records the sources and each change Diana asked for. |
| `outputs/posts/ready/linkedin/05-subscription-or-usage-pricing.md` | An approved post, not posted yet: a pricing question for AI products, built on GitHub's change and a pricing survey, with a two-page carousel and its X, Threads and Bluesky set. |
| `outputs/posts/ready/linkedin/06-google-ads-still-work.md` | An approved question to founders, from the idea in `ideas/used/2026-10-09-google-ads.md`, with its X, Threads and Bluesky set. |
| `outputs/posts/ready/linkedin/07-ai-text-detectable.md` | An approved post planned for the EU deadline (`planned_for`), with a two-page carousel and its X, Threads and Bluesky set. |
| `outputs/visuals/subscription-or-usage-pricing/carousel/`, `outputs/visuals/ai-text-detectable/carousel/` | Two-page carousels made with the carousel kit: a meme page, then a page of checked facts. `slide-1.html` and `slide-2.html` are the sources; the PDF goes to LinkedIn and the `-1080` images to the other channels. |
| `outputs/visuals/character-ai-bankruptcy/` | A visual from a primary source: `build.py` crops the quoted lines from the court PDFs, unchanged, and highlights them. |
| `outputs/visuals/content-workflow/` | The README diagram in the brand style, as editable HTML and its PNG. |
| `ideas/open/` | Two raw ideas, saved word for word. |
| `outputs/angles.md` | An angle cut from post 03, kept for a follow-up. |

The memes in the carousels and the Google Ads post are third-party images, credited in the post files and slide sources. Two other third-party visuals (a meme clip and a quote card) aren't included. The logos in the Figma → React animation belong to their companies. The ideas keep Diana's notes exactly as typed, typos included. Creators' names are left out.
